Illustrative. Growth is measured on the returns the owner already files with the city or county, so nobody has to take our word for it. It takes time: a better quarter shows on the next return. We cannot promise a price, a multiple or a sale.

Why it is easy for you
  • It costs you nothing. There is no fee to the broker.
  • It costs the owner nothing up front. We earn 25 percent of the increase in room revenue for twelve months. If it does not grow, the owner owes nothing.
  • You stay the broker. We do not list, sell or value property, and we do not come between you and your owner.
  • You see the numbers move. With the owner's okay, we send you a short note each time a return is filed.
