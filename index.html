Common questions

Plain answers.

Something not covered here? Email adam@theinnkeeperagency.com and Adam answers himself.

What does The Innkeeper Agency do?

We manage revenue for independent motels, inns, lodges and cabins with about 6 to 30 rooms: nightly rates, listings and photos on the booking sites, a direct booking website, and working to make the property show up when travelers ask an AI assistant where to stay.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. Under the pilot terms for our first ten owners, we earn 25 percent of the increase in room revenue over the same months of the year before, for twelve months. If revenue does not grow, you owe nothing for our time. Outside costs, such as a local photographer, are yours to pay only when you approve them first.

How is growth measured?

On the transient occupancy tax returns you already file with the city or county. Nobody has to take our word for the result.

What stays with the owner?

Everything about running the place: guests, staff, cleaning, the phone and the front desk. Every payment goes straight to your own accounts, and the website, photos and listings stay in your name.

What access do you need?

A user login to the booking sites, the reservation program, the website and the Google listing. You keep ownership of every account, and we never see or change bank or payout details.

What happens after twelve months?

The pilot ends. The website, the domain, the photos and every account are in your name and stay there. If both sides want to continue, we write a new agreement.

Can the owner end it early?

Yes. Either side can end the pilot with written notice. The agreement spells out the notice period and what is owed on growth already achieved, and you read it before deciding anything.

Do you work with brokers?

Yes. If you list small lodging and have an owner whose income does not yet support the price they want, we can help raise the numbers a buyer and a lender look at. It costs the broker nothing. See the page for brokers.
