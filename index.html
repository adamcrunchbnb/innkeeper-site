Common questions

Plain answers.

What does The Innkeeper Agency do?

We manage revenue for independent motels, inns, lodges and cabins with about 6 to 30 rooms. That means setting nightly rates, keeping listings and photos current on the booking sites, building a direct booking website, and making sure the property shows up when travelers ask an AI assistant where to stay.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. We earn 25 percent of the increase in room revenue over the same months of the year before, for twelve months. If revenue does not grow, the owner owes nothing for our time. Outside costs, such as a local photographer, are paid by the owner only when approved first.

How is growth measured?

On the transient occupancy tax returns the owner already files with the city or county. Nobody has to take our word for the result.

What stays with the owner?

Everything about running the place: guests, staff, cleaning, the phone and the front desk. Every payment goes straight to the owner's own accounts, and the website, photos and listings stay in the owner's name.

Do you work with brokers?

Yes. If you list small lodging and have an owner whose income does not yet support the price they want, we can help raise the numbers a buyer and a lender look at. It costs the broker nothing.
