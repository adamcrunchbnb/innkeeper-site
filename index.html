The deal
  • Nothing up front. No setup fee and no monthly retainer.
  • Paid only on growth. I earn 25 percent of the increase in your room revenue for twelve months. If it does not grow, you owe nothing.
  • Measured on your own numbers. The occupancy tax returns you already file, against the same months last year.
  • In dollars. If your rooms brought in $90,000 last summer and $102,000 this summer, the increase is $12,000. I bill $3,000 and you keep the other $9,000. That is an example of the arithmetic, not a forecast.
  • You stay in control. Your guests, staff, bank accounts, website and listings all stay yours.

Common questions

Plain answers.

What does The Innkeeper Agency do?

I set the nightly rates, keep the listings and photos current on the booking sites, build a direct booking website, and work to get the property named when travelers ask an AI assistant where to stay. It is for independent motels, inns, lodges and cabins with about 6 to 30 rooms.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. I earn 25 percent of the increase in room revenue over the same months of the year before, for twelve months. If revenue does not grow, you owe nothing for my time. Outside costs, such as a local photographer, are yours only when you approve them first.

How is growth measured?

On the transient occupancy tax returns you already file with the city or county. Nobody has to take my word for the result.

What stays with the owner?

Everything about running the place: guests, staff, cleaning, the phone and the front desk. Every payment goes straight to your own accounts, and the website, photos and listings stay in your name, during the twelve months and after.

Do you work with brokers?

Yes. If you list small lodging and have an owner whose income does not yet support the price they want, I work on the room revenue a buyer and a lender look at. It takes time, because growth shows on the next tax returns. It costs the broker nothing.
